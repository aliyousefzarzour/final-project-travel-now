Who We Are

Driven by Purpose

The Problem

Travelers visiting Egypt face last-minute cancellations, hidden fees, poor communication with operators, weak complaint handling, and a lack of an organized, reliable platform to find accurate information and trusted services.

Our Solution

A seamless, all-in-one web platform connecting tourists with local Egyptian agencies — featuring an interactive map, searchable historical guide, verified company listings, and Yala Plan smart trip planner. No more text-heavy lists.

Our Impact

For users: saves time and ensures confident trip planning. For businesses: direct promotion boosts local tour company sales. For the economy: drives overall tourism growth and job creation across Egypt.

What We Solve

Project Challenges

Last-minute trip cancellations causing major inconvenience for travelers.
Unclear pricing and hidden extra fees charged by operators.
Poor communication with tour operators, delaying booking confirmations.
Technical issues with online booking and identity/payment verification.
Weak complaint-handling during the trip and lack of transparency in official channels.

How We Got Here

2023

The Idea Sparks

A group of passionate Egyptians saw a gap — world-class destinations, but no unified digital platform. TravelNow was born in a Cairo café.

Beta launch with 50+ attractions and 20 verified partners. First 1,000 users onboarded in the first month of operation.
